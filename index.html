About me

Hi, I’m Deven Makwana, a passionate and detail-oriented UI/UX Designer at the beginning of my professional journey. As a fresher, I bring a strong foundation in user-centered design and a deep enthusiasm for creating digital experiences that are not only visually appealing but also functional and intuitive. I’ve worked on several academic and personal projects where I practiced the full design process—from conducting user research and creating personas to wireframing, prototyping, and usability testing.

My skill set includes user research, information architecture, interaction design, responsive and mobile-first design, accessibility, and usability analysis.I’m proficient with tools like Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, Illustrator, Photoshop, Miro, and platforms like Notion, Trello, and Maze for testing and collaboration. I love turning complex problems into simple, elegant solutions and enjoy collaborating with developers, designers, and product teams to bring ideas to life.

Always curious and constantly learning, I stay updated with the latest design trends and tools to grow as a designer. I’m excited to create experiences that not only look great but also feel meaningful and user-friendly.
